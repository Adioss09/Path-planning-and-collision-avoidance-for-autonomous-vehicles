# Results

This document is populated after running the scenarios in the simulation.
Metrics are output to `results/metrics/`.

### Evaluated Scenarios:
1. **Unmarked Village Road:** Vehicle successfully maintains safe trajectory despite no clear lanes.
2. **Busy Urban Intersection:** Vehicle calculates risk of cross traffic and yields/replans.
3. **Dense Market Area:** High volume of dynamic obstacles (pedestrians/carts) forces continuous replanning and speed reduction.
4. **Sudden Cattle Crossing:** Ego vehicle detects animal, computes critical TTC, and successfully triggers an emergency brake or sharp evasive maneuver.
